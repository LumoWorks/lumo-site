Wedding Ready 30 隱私權政策

生效日：2026-07-17

摘要

Wedding Ready 30 不要求帳號、不使用分析追蹤，也不將你的計畫資料傳送至 Lumo。你的計畫資料只儲存在你的裝置沙盒中。

我們不蒐集的資料

我們不蒐集姓名、聯絡資料、照片、位置、健康資料、體重、尺寸、相機或相簿資料；本 App 也不使用 analytics 或廣告追蹤。

裝置上的資料

App 在你的裝置上保存顯示名稱、婚期、每日時間偏好、在意事項、任務完成狀態與 Event Pass entitlement 所需的最少非敏感紀錄。你可在 Data 頁自行匯出 JSON 或確認後清除準備資料。

商店購買

Event Pass 是一次性 App 內購買。Apple App Store 或 Google Play 依其各自的隱私權條款處理付款與購買紀錄；Lumo 不取得付款卡資料。

兒童與健康

本 App 不提供健康診斷、治療、快速減重、治痘、飲食或運動處方，亦不保證任何效果。

Wedding Ready 30 Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-17

Summary

Wedding Ready 30 does not require an account, use analytics, or send your plan data to Lumo. Your plan stays only in your device sandbox.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect names, contact details, photos, location, health data, weight, measurements, camera or photo-library data. The app does not use analytics or advertising tracking.

On-device data

The app stores only your display name, wedding date, daily-time preference, concerns, task state, and the minimum non-sensitive Event Pass entitlement record on your device. You can export JSON or confirm a reset from Data.

Store purchases

Event Pass is a one-time in-app purchase. Apple App Store or Google Play processes payment and purchase records under its own privacy terms. Lumo does not receive payment-card data.

Children and health

The app does not provide health diagnosis, treatment, rapid weight loss, acne treatment, diet or exercise prescriptions, or guaranteed results.
