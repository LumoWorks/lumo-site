我們的工作方式。How we work.

不先堆功能。先找最短路徑，做出能讓人判斷價值的版本。No feature pileups. We find the shortest path to something people can judge.

  • 先驗證Validate first先做能看、能問、能收回饋的東西。Build something that can be seen, asked about, and improved.
  • 少維護Low maintenance能用一頁、一份清單、一個簡單流程解決，就不做大型系統。If one page, one checklist, or one workflow works, we do not build a platform.
  • 人類把關Human approval對外聯絡、公開上線、付款、合約與承諾，都需要人類確認。Outreach, public launch, payment, contracts, and commitments need human approval.
